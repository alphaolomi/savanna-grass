### Your day, in full
Tap the grass-debt card (**Hour by hour, apps and the maths ›**) to open the day:
- **The debt, worked out.** Your screen time × 10 min an hour, what distracting time added, the 60-minute cap if it applied, minus your time outside. Every line uses that day's numbers, so you can check the figure yourself.
- **Hour by hour.** When you were on screens, split into productive, neutral and distracting, with a leaf under every hour you spent outside.
- **How the time was spent.** Your productivity mix and Pulse, your top apps (with category and how often you went back to them), and your categories.
- **Time outside.** That day's breaks; tap one to open it.

### Your week
Tap **This week outside** for the last seven days: how many days you paid off, minutes earned against minutes outside for each day, and a row per day that opens it. You can also tap a single day's bar on the home screen.

### Each break
Tap a break to see when it was, what you noticed, which day's debt it paid (split between two days if it ran past midnight), and whether it has reached SavannaTime yet.
