**Your break survives your pocket.** Savanna Grass now keeps track of the day, the break and your reminders when the phone is locked, the app is closed, or midnight passes while you are outside.

### Breaks you can trust
- **A break is saved the moment it starts.** If Android closes the app while you are outside (common on phones with 4 GB of RAM, with Twiga loaded), reopening it brings you straight back to the running timer, with your note.
- **A timer in your notifications** keeps counting with the app closed. Tap it to come back and log the break.
- **Quest-time alarm.** Your phone rings when the break reaches its target, and checks in later if you are still out.
- **Midnight is handled.** A break from 23:50 to 00:10 pays ten minutes of each day's debt. At midnight the app starts the new day by itself, without showing yesterday's screen time as today's.
- One break counts for up to 3 hours, so a phone forgotten in a jacket doesn't log a night outside. Cancelling a real break now asks first.

### Reminders
- **Daily nudge** about your grass debt, around 5:30 PM by default (change it in **Profile → Reminders**). It is skipped on days you have already paid, and while you are outside.
- Reminders are scheduled on the phone and survive a restart. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Android asks for notification permission at your first break. For alarms on the exact minute, allow **Alarms & reminders** when the app offers it; without it, Android may ring a few minutes late, and the daily nudge can arrive up to an hour after its time.

### Profile
- Tap your avatar for your **Profile**: name, email, member since, and your week outside (minutes, streak, breaks).
- **Set your name.** It is saved to your SavannaTime account, and the greeting uses it.
- **Time-zone check.** If your SavannaTime account counts days in a different zone from your phone, Profile tells you, because then "today" starts at different hours in each.
- Theme, reminders, Twiga's status and sign-out live here now. Signing out tells you first if any breaks haven't synced.

### Steadier
- Comes back with fresh numbers when you return to the app, and renews your sign-in before it expires.
- Opens with today's last known numbers when you have no signal, instead of zeros.
- Fixed: a break finished while another was syncing could be counted twice, or dropped from the queue.
- Fixed: numbers from a refresh still in flight when you signed out could appear after sign-out.
